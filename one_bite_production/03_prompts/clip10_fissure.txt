SCENE CONTEXT
The inspection begins. Above Seoul, the vast black sphere that has hung motionless for 43 days finally moves: a horizontal fissure splits open across its face like a colossal mouth, glowing from within, and a voice the whole planet can hear declares the seven-day judgment of Earth.

ACTION
The sphere hangs sealed for a breath. Then a hairline seam of golden light draws itself horizontally across its face and yawns open into a vast lip-shaped fissure — no teeth, no tongue — its interior lined with hundreds of tiny red lenses that ignite in ripples, all focusing downward. Thin clouds around it recoil outward in a slow ring.

PERFORMANCE
No human characters. The sphere performs: unhurried, administrative, absolutely indifferent — a cosmic official opening a case file.
